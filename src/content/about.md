I'm Ty, or Ed.

I'm interested in political economy, individualist anarchism, radical liberalism, free markets, globalization, decentralized technology, audio, and building weird things on the web.

Politically, I describe myself as a globalist philosophical anarchist and pragmatic liberal free enterpriser. My outlook centers on individual liberty, equal citizenship, free association, open trade, cosmopolitanism, and skepticism toward concentrated power.

I build and experiment with projects around political ideology and measurement, AT Protocol, decentralized publishing, web design, and other internet infrastructure. I also spend an unreasonable amount of time thinking about headphones, HRTFs, old web interfaces, and obscure political thinkers.

This site is where I put longer thoughts, research, project notes, and whatever else I'm working on.
